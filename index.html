Design Requirements (Client Specifications)

Brand Identity

  • Business Name: Spec & Harmony
  • Logo: NEEDS TO BE DESIGNED - modern, minimal, premium aesthetic
  • Brand Colors: Crisp white background ONLY - products must stand out against pure white
  • Typography: Modern, clean, premium (use Apple as reference)
  • Overall Aesthetic: Not cluttered, easy to look at, minimal and breathable

Specific Design Rules

Navigation/Menu Bar

  • Logo positioned on the left side of the menu bar (Apple style)
  • Clean, minimal menu bar design
  • Reference: Apple.com navigation placement and style

Homepage Product Cards

  • Products displayed with balanced composition - both the product AND the background image should stand out
  • Cards should have a subtle shadow to create depth (make them look "forward and alive")
  • Clean, premium look

Collection Page Product Cards

  • Show ONLY the product (no background, no context)
  • No floating effects or unnecessary animations
  • Simple, clean product presentation
  • Maintain shadow for depth/dimension

Product Images

  • All products on white/transparent backgrounds
  • High-quality, professional presentation
  • Clean product photography style

Overall Layout Requirements

  • Generous white space (like Apple.com)
  • Not cluttered - "essentials only" feel on homepage
  • Well-organized catalog structure underneath
  • Aligned elements, premium typography
  • Mobile-first responsive design

Design References

Primary Reference: Apple.com

  • Minimalism and white space
  • Clean typography
  • Menu bar placement and logo positioning
  • Product presentation style

Secondary Reference: Moogmusic.com

  • Premium audio equipment positioning
  • Professional product photography
  • BUT use white background instead of dark

Aesthetic Goals:

  • Luxury but approachable
  • Professional but not corporate
  • Modern but timeless
  • Clean but not sterile
